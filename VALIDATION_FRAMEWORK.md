# DBA Compliance Check - Validation vs Building Framework

## Executive Summary

This document captures the critical thinking framework for balancing validation and building in the DBA Compliance Check project. After extensive analysis, we've determined that the project has sufficient validation signals to proceed with shipping the MVP and learning through real user interaction.

## The Validation vs Building Dilemma

### Problem Statement
Entrepreneurs face a constant tension between:
- **Building without validation**: Wastes time/money on unwanted products
- **Endless validation**: Never ships, misses market opportunities

### Our Solution
**80% validation complete → 20% validation through building**

- **Market signals**: Confirmed DBA fear and recruiter activity

- **Basic pricing**: Internal testing of €49-149 value propositions

### 🎯 Remaining Validation (20%)

- Qualitative feedback loops

## Decision Criteria: When to Stop Validating

### Ship Now Triggers
- [x] Working MVP exists (not just prototype)
- [x] Market shows real pain signals (DBA fear confirmed)
- [x] Competition is reacting (midlance boom)
- [x] Shipping cost is low (< 1 week effort)
- [x] Fast iteration capability (code changes quick)

### Validate More Triggers
- [ ] No working prototype
- [ ] Market signals are weak/hypothetical
- [ ] High regulatory/legal risk
- [ ] Massive development cost
- [ ] Complex technical architecture

## Go/No-Go Decision Framework

### Go Criteria (Proceed to Build)
- 50+ form completions from ZZP'er communities
- 25%+ completion rate
- Professional email quality
- Positive qualitative feedback
- 3+ expressions of willingness to pay

### Caution Criteria (Validate More)
- 50+ completions but low engagement
- Traffic from non-target sources
- No willingness to pay signals
- Mixed/borderline feedback

### Stop Criteria (Pivot/Abandon)
- <30 completions after 2 weeks
- Zero engagement from communities
- All feedback: "Not concerned about DBA"
- Strong negative signals
